## Design Context

### Users
Long-term cohabiting couples who want to balance mental load and household chores in a practical, low-friction way. They mainly use the app in short daily check-ins to define recurring tasks, mark completion quickly, and verify current balance at a glance.

Primary job to be done:
- Define tasks (mostly recurring), complete them quickly, and understand the current load balance without emotional friction.

### Brand Personality
3-word personality:
- Team
- Calm
- Clarity

Voice and tone:
- Cooperative and plural-first ("we", "together")
- Practical utility over sentimental fluff
- Encouraging accountability without guilt

Emotional goals:
- Calm
- Accountability

Explicit anti-direction:
- Do not resemble a love-note-focused couple app with little utility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Current product direction inferred from codebase:
- Mobile-first dark interface with emerald as primary accent.
- Rounded cards/surfaces, soft-glow highlights, and dense but readable information grouping.
- Friendly, contemporary typography using Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Utility-led interaction patterns: quick actions, sticky nav, visible state banners, and clear status semantics.

Theme preference:
- Dark mode first.

References:
- No explicit reference products provided yet.

Accessibility:
- No explicit WCAG target or accommodation requirements provided yet.

### Design Principles
1. Make teamwork visible: prioritize shared outcomes and "we" framing over individual scoring narratives.
2. Optimize for daily speed: common flows (create recurring task, complete task, check balance) should be one-glance or one-tap where possible.
3. Keep accountability non-punitive: surface imbalance clearly with calm language and supportive cues, never accusatory tone.
4. Favor clarity over decoration: every visual element should help users decide or act faster.
5. Preserve dark-first legibility: maintain high contrast, predictable hierarchy, and accessible state signaling in low-light usage.
